## Full description
LudoMax is a fast, fun mini Ludo game that works completely offline — no internet, no sign-up, no ads.

🎲 Classic Ludo rules: roll a 6 to leave your yard, capture opponents, use safe stars, and bring both tokens home with an exact roll.
🤖 Play vs Computer (Easy or Hard) or Pass & Play with 2–4 players on one phone.
✨ Smooth animations, bouncing tokens, rolling dice, confetti for the winner.
🎵 Cheerful music and sound effects (you can switch them off any time).
🔒 Privacy first: LudoMax collects no personal data.

Download LudoMax and enjoy a quick game anytime, anywhere!
