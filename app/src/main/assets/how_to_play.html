How to Play

LudoMax • Quick rules
Goal: bring both of your pawns around the board and into the centre before your opponents do.

Players & colours

Play with 2, 3 or 4 players — against the computer or with friends on one phone. Each player has 2 pawns. Green Red Blue Yellow.

Your turn

  • Tap your dice to roll it.
  • You need a 6 to bring a pawn out of its home yard onto your coloured start square.
  • Pawns move clockwise by the number on the dice. If you have two possible moves, tap the pawn you want to move (the glowing pawns can move). If only one move is possible, it is played for you.
  • If no pawn can move, the turn passes to the next player.

Bonus turns

  • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll.
  • Capturing an opponent’s pawn gives you another roll.
  • Bringing a pawn home gives you another roll.
  • Three 6s in a row cancel your turn.

Capturing

If you land exactly on a square occupied by a single opponent pawn, that pawn is sent back to its yard and must wait for a 6 again.

Safe squares

  • The four coloured start squares (with a white star) are always safe.
  • The four grey star squares are safe too.
  • Two pawns of the same colour on one square protect each other.
  • Your coloured home column is safe — opponents cannot enter it.

Going home

After a full lap your pawn turns into your coloured home column at the arrow. You must roll the exact number needed to reach the centre.

Winning

The first player to bring both pawns to the centre wins. In 3–4 player games the others keep playing for 2nd and 3rd place.

Tip: Use the music and sound buttons at the top of the game screen, or the pause button for restart options.
